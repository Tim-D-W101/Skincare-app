# Calibration photos

Put 15 to 20 photos here for `npm run calibrate`. Everything in this folder
except this file is gitignored. **Never commit real faces.** Use only photos
of people who have agreed to it.

A useful mix:

- Varied skin tones, ages and skin types.
- Different lighting: daylight, indoor, warm and cool light.
- A few deliberately bad ones, to check rejection: too dark, blurry, no face,
  two faces, face far from the camera.

For results that match real scans, take the photos with the app's camera,
or resize them to about 1024 px on the long edge. The app sends JPEGs of that
size, under 500 KB. The report flags much larger files.
